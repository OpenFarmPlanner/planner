# Critical workflow test coverage audit · 2026-11-23

Audited `main` at `f78a5e59a4c9e61e42740868cc73371b373812f2`, the merge of the crop bulk-selection change. The audit follows the major write paths and samples the assertions that would fail if a request, state transition or persistence step broke. “Adequate” applies to the paths named in each row. [Audited revision](https://github.com/OpenFarmPlanner/planner/commit/f78a5e59a4c9e61e42740868cc73371b373812f2) · [Issue #1486](https://github.com/OpenFarmPlanner/planner/issues/1486) · [Testing and CI](testing-and-ci.md) · [QA strategy](qa-strategy.md) · [Repository rules](../CLAUDE.md)

Required CI exercises real imports and retries, crop and plan saves, season copy and project-version restore. The most consequential uncovered transitions are a new account submitting a moderated crop edit and deleting a live project through the trash. Empty-project onboarding also stops short of submitting its own form. The follow-ups call for one small Django API test, one Chromium scenario and completion of an existing Chromium case.

The closest database-backed cases cover location cascade and bed deletion with history replay. The open growing-area undo issue already includes a field-delete recovery workflow.
